\section{Bug fix}
The reverse Ubuntu-to-iPad TCP transport now enables aggressive TCP keepalive and
a bounded Linux user timeout so half-open sockets are discarded promptly. Explicit
image-open requests are no longer considered delivered immediately after writing
to a socket. Delivery is confirmed only after the connection survives the health
window; otherwise the durable request remains pending and is resent automatically
after the next reverse connection is established.
