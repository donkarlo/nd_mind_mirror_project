   \begin{preview}
      \section{Bug}
      Relying on QMenu selection/highlight palette state allowed an old hovered
      item to retain its purple background, and platform palette groups could
      still render selected text white.

      \section{Fix}
      Hover is now represented only by the exact QAction geometry under the
      pointer. Every menu-item paint explicitly fills either the light-purple
      hover background or the original menu background. Switching rows or
      leaving the menu synchronously repaints the whole small popup, so stale
      hover color cannot survive. Text and highlighted-text roles are forced to
      black for active, inactive, and disabled palette groups.
   \end{preview}
