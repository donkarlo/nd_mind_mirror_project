\section*{Structure title filter and deep LaTeX headings --- 2026-10-10}
\subsection*{Structure title search}
Added a debounced search field above the desktop Structure tree. The filter
accepts partial heading titles and small typographical errors in Latin and
Persian text. Matching headings retain their ancestor chain and all unrelated
branches are hidden without modifying LaTeX or YAML. Pressing Enter navigates
to the first matching heading; clearing the field restores the full tree and
previous expansion state.

\subsection*{Deep heading detection}
Recognizes the custom section levels defined in
\texttt{nd\_language\_ai\_project/.../code\_clips/deep\_structure.tex},
including \texttt{DeepSection}, \texttt{ndsectionfour} through
\texttt{ndsectionten}, and long \texttt{sub...section} aliases from
\texttt{extra\_subsections.tex}. The supplied deep\_structure.tex has
10 section levels and separately configures 20 \texttt{itemize} levels.
Deep headings and subordinate images/tables now appear at their correct
semantic Structure depths. Unsupported deep-heading source rewrites remain
explicitly blocked rather than emitting invalid LaTeX.
