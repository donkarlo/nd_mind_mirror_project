      \section*{2026-10-01 --- iPad quick-tool double-tap disambiguation fix}

      The Apple Pencil quick-tool gesture was adjusted to remain easy to activate
      without treating fast handwriting strokes as a double tap.

      \begin{itemize}
         \item The application-level double-tap limits are 0.35 seconds and 12 points.
         \item Pencil and highlighter activation additionally validates the PencilKit
               strokes created by the gesture.
         \item Only one or two newly added, dot-like strokes are accepted as a
               quick-tool gesture; letter- or line-shaped strokes are rejected.
         \item Validation runs on the next main run-loop turn so the second PencilKit
               stroke is present before the decision is made.
         \item A rejected gesture leaves the user's drawing untouched.
      \end{itemize}
